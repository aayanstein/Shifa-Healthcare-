# 1. PROJECT IDENTITY

Project name: **SHIFA.AI**

Core tagline:

> **AI-Powered Healthcare At Your Fingertips**

SHIFA is a Bangladesh-focused AI-powered healthcare platform / healthcare companion.

The core idea is to organize a patient's healthcare information into one digital ecosystem so that patients can understand their information more easily and authorized doctors can understand previous medical history more efficiently.

---

- Bangla + English healthcare explanation

- Expert doctor suggestions

The platform is intended to make healthcare information easier to manage before, during, and after receiving healthcare.

# 4. AI SHIFA

AI SHIFA is the healthcare AI assistant.

Intended capabilities:

- Explain healthcare information.
- Explain medical reports in simpler language.
- Summarize available medical history.
- Answer healthcare-information questions.
- Support Bangla and English.
- Use the user's available records/context when appropriate.
- Help patients understand information before discussing it with a professional.

Example:

User:
> Can you explain my blood test report?

AI SHIFA:
> Explains the report in understandable language.

User:
> আমার রিপোর্টটা বাংলায় বুঝিয়ে দিতে পারবে?

AI SHIFA:
> Responds in understandable Bangla.

IMPORTANT:
AI SHIFA should be presented as a healthcare information/assistance tool, not as a replacement for a licensed doctor or emergency care.
